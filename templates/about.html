About LexAI

Legal & Cyber Law Intelligence Platform

LexAI is an AI-powered Legal & Cyber Law Intelligence Platform designed to simplify legal research using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), semantic search, and Large Language Models.

Instead of relying solely on AI-generated knowledge, LexAI retrieves relevant information directly from official Indian legal Acts, cyber security regulations, and government guidelines before generating every answer.

Project Overview

Bridging Artificial Intelligence and Indian Legal Knowledge through Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

What is LexAI?

LexAI is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) application that combines semantic search, vector databases, and Large Language Models to provide accurate, citation-based legal responses from trusted Indian legal documents.

Every answer is generated using relevant legal context retrieved from the knowledge base, improving reliability while reducing hallucinations.

Why LexAI?

  • Trusted Government Documents
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation
  • Citation-Based Responses
  • Semantic Search
  • Local AI Inference using Llama 3

Our Mission

To make Indian legal and cyber law information easier to understand by combining trusted legal documents with modern Artificial Intelligence.

LexAI enables students, researchers, developers, and cyber security professionals to access legal information quickly while maintaining transparency through document citations.

Our Vision

To build an intelligent legal assistant capable of providing reliable, explainable, and citation-backed legal guidance using Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

LexAI demonstrates how AI can responsibly support legal research while grounding every response in authentic government publications.

Why Retrieval-Augmented Generation?

Traditional AI models generate responses primarily from previously learned knowledge, which may not always reflect the latest regulations or official legal documents.

LexAI combines semantic retrieval with Large Language Models by first searching relevant legal documents stored in its knowledge base and then generating responses using that retrieved context.

This approach significantly improves factual accuracy, reduces AI hallucinations, and provides transparent, citation-supported answers.

Benefits of RAG

  • Grounded in official legal documents
  • Citation-based responses
  • Reduced AI hallucinations
  • Reliable semantic retrieval
  • Explainable AI responses
